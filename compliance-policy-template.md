## 1. Purpose and scope

[Organization] maintains a documented process for identifying applicable obligations, assigning responsibility, operating controls, and resolving deficiencies.

This policy applies to [legal entities, activities, products, jurisdictions, staff, and third parties within scope]. [Role] maintains the scope record and documents exclusions and their rationale. A material change in business activity, jurisdiction, ownership, or regulatory authority triggers a scope review.

## 2. Governing requirements

[Role] maintains an obligations register connecting each applicable requirement to its primary authority, relevant section, affected process, responsible owner, and review date. The register distinguishes statutes, final rules, proposed rules, guidance, contractual duties, and adopted internal standards.

When interpreting a judicial decision, the reviewer records the controlling holding, the procedural posture, the remedy, and material scope limitations. An uncertain applicability question is assigned to [appropriate reviewer] and remains open until the decision and rationale are documented.

## 3. Responsibility and reporting

The responsibility matrix records each material activity and the roles accountable, responsible, consulted, and informed. Each activity has one accountable role. The board or delegated committee receives [defined reporting package] on [adopted cadence] and upon [material escalation triggers].

The executive sponsor allocates resources and resolves ownership conflicts. Compliance coordinates the obligations and risk registers. Process owners operate assigned controls and retain evidence. Independent assurance evaluates controls according to [approved scope and independence arrangements].

## 4. Regulatory change assessment

For each relevant development, the reviewer records the official source, event date, source review date, current procedural status, and the organization's applicability question. The assigned owner evaluates affected products, processes, policies, controls, training, and reporting.

The owner records an applicability decision with supporting reasoning, required actions, approval authority, and a review date. A proposed rule or interim order is identified as such. The record separates a legal effective date from an internal implementation target. Change assessment closes only after the disposition and evidence are recorded.

## 5. Risk assessment and control design

The approved likelihood scale is [definitions]. The approved impact scale is [definitions]. The organization evaluates inherent risk before controls and residual risk after examining control design and operating evidence. The documented assessment includes [required rationale and evidence reference].

Each control identifies its objective, operator, frequency or trigger, required inputs, steps, output evidence, reviewer, and exception path. Where evidence is missing, the assessment states that limitation and assigns follow-up work. [Authority] approves risk acceptance within [defined delegation and limits].

## 6. Issues and escalation

Staff record suspected control deficiencies through [reporting channel]. The recipient records the issue, affected process, source information, immediate containment, owner, and next action. Escalation to [role or body] occurs upon [defined materiality or urgency trigger].

The issue owner maintains progress and evidence references. Any change to a target date records the reason, authorizing role, and revised date. Closure requires verification that the corrective action addresses the identified deficiency, with the approver and remaining follow-up recorded.

## 7. Exceptions

An exception request identifies the requirement, reason, affected scope, alternative control, duration or review trigger, owner, and risk assessment. Approval occurs only within [delegated authority]. The exception record includes conditions, evidence expectations, and the decision's expiry or review date.

Exceptions do not alter an external obligation. Where an external requirement cannot be met, [appropriate reviewer] determines the available response and records the authority supporting it.

## 8. Evidence and records

Owners retain the evidence identified in each control description. Records include the covered period, creation date, preparer, reviewer where applicable, source location, and decision supported. Access, retention, disposal, and legal-hold handling follow [approved schedule and controls].

Evidence references must allow an authorized reviewer to locate the material without placing sensitive records into public documents. The review log preserves substantive findings and decisions; later reviews are new entries linked to the affected record.

## 9. Training and assurance

[Role] identifies training needs from job responsibilities, applicable changes, and observed deficiencies. Completion evidence records the relevant content and period. Independent assurance uses [approved methodology] to test control design and operation, report findings, and verify corrective action.

## 10. Review and approval

This policy is reviewed on [adopted cadence] and following [material change triggers]. The policy owner records revisions, consulted roles, approval, effective date, and affected implementation work.
