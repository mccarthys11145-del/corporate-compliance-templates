Use the checklist to establish what supports an assessment or closure decision. Mark an item complete only after locating the evidence. For an inapplicable item, record the reason.

## Requirement and scope

- [ ] Primary authority or adopted internal requirement is identified with a section or pinpoint.
- [ ] Applicable entity, jurisdiction, process, product, and period are recorded.
- [ ] The record distinguishes final requirements, proposals, guidance, and interim orders.
- [ ] Material later amendments, orders, or decisions have been considered.
- [ ] The applicability decision and reviewer are documented.

## Responsibility and control design

- [ ] Accountable and responsible roles match the approved delegation.
- [ ] The control objective and requirement are connected.
- [ ] The control frequency or trigger, inputs, procedure, evidence, and exception path are defined.
- [ ] Any conflicts or independence requirements are addressed in the allocation.

## Control operation

- [ ] Evidence identifies when the control ran and the period it covers.
- [ ] The preparer and reviewer, where required, are identifiable.
- [ ] The evidence supports the recorded result, including exceptions.
- [ ] The sample selection or coverage method is recorded where testing uses a sample.
- [ ] Missing evidence is recorded as an open item with an owner.

## Risk and corrective action

- [ ] Likelihood and impact use the organization's defined scale.
- [ ] Residual assessment reflects control evidence and documented limitations.
- [ ] Each required action has an owner, intended result, and approved target or trigger.
- [ ] Changes to targets or scope include a rationale and authorization record.
- [ ] Risk acceptance or an exception identifies the approving authority and review conditions.

## Closure and retention

- [ ] Implementation evidence demonstrates the action taken.
- [ ] Verification addresses the original issue and records any remaining concern.
- [ ] The closure decision, approver, and date are recorded.
- [ ] Remaining follow-up is assigned and remains visible.
- [ ] Evidence is accessible to authorized reviewers and retained under the applicable schedule or hold.
- [ ] The review log links this assessment to the affected record and prior decisions.
